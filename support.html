Frequently asked questions

How do I log an expense?

Tap plus at the bottom, type the amount on the number pad and tap the category. The entry is saved with today's date and the last used account. You can add a note, payee or receipt photo before tapping the category, or edit the entry later. If you prefer to pick the category first, type the amount afterwards and tap "Save".

How do budgets work?

In the Budgets tab you set an amount per month for a category, plus an optional total budget. The bar turns yellow at 80% and red at 100%. Budgets apply to every month until you change them. Two budgets are free, Premium removes the limit.

What are recurring entries?

Rent, subscriptions or salary are set up once as a rule: amount, category, account, repeat (weekly, monthly, yearly, also every two or three), start and end. On the due date an entry is created. Until then it appears in the list as planned, so nothing counts as done that is still outstanding. Recurring entries are part of Premium.

How does the receipt scan work?

When adding an entry, tap the scan icon and photograph the receipt. Text recognition runs on your device and suggests total, date and merchant, and for well-known chains the category too. You check, adjust if needed and save. The photo stays attached to the entry. Your receipt never leaves your phone.

What are accounts and transfers?

Accounts are cash, checking account, credit card, savings or whatever you need. Every entry belongs to an account, and the balance is the opening balance plus entries. A transfer moves money between two accounts, for example withdrawing cash. One account is free, Premium allows as many as you like.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, no bank connection and no server. Text recognition for receipts runs on the device as well. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I back up my data or switch phones?

Under More › "Create backup" you save everything into one file, optionally encrypted with a password, for example to iCloud Drive or via AirDrop to your computer. On the new device choose "Restore backup".

Can I use the data in Excel?

Yes. Under More you export all entries as an Excel file with three sheets (entries, accounts, months) or as CSV.

What does the app cost?

Entries, categories, one account, two budgets, the monthly overview, backup and export are free, without ads and without a limit on entries. Premium adds unlimited accounts and budgets, recurring entries, receipt scanning, the year overview with review and Face ID. You pay yearly (with a seven-day trial) or once. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.

How do I delete my data?

Under More › "Delete all data", or by deleting the app. Since nothing is stored on servers, that removes everything.
