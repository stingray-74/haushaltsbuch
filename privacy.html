Privacy Policy

for the app "Haushaltsbuch" (iOS)

In short: Everything you enter in Haushaltsbuch stays on your device. There is no user account, no bank connection, no cloud, no advertising and no analysis of your behaviour. We never see your entries, accounts, budgets or receipts. Even the text recognition for receipts runs on your device.

2. What data the app processes

The app stores the data you enter yourself: entries (amount, date, category, account, payee, note), receipt photos, accounts with opening balance, categories, budgets, rules for recurring entries and your settings (language, currency, colour scheme, reminder time, lock, Premium status). This data is kept exclusively in a local database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties. The app does not access bank accounts and does not connect to banks.

Insofar as any processing by us takes place at all, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the app). Local storage on your device does not require any processing by us.

4. Receipt recognition on the device

When you photograph a receipt, the app reads the total, date and merchant from the photo and suggests them to you. For this it uses Google's ML Kit library (text recognition), which works entirely on your device. Your photos and the recognised text are not sent to Google. However, the library may occasionally contact Google servers to download bug fixes, updated models and device compatibility information, and it sends technical metrics about the library's performance and usage to Google. This may involve device information and usage statistics, never content. Details in the ML Kit Terms and Google's Privacy Policy. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working and secure text recognition (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Purchases (Premium)

You buy Premium through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data, no name and no address. Apple's privacy policy applies.

To verify and manage purchases we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This involves a random, anonymous user identifier, the store's purchase receipts and technical details such as app version, device type and system language. This identifier is not linked to your name. RevenueCat is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract).

6. Backup and export

You create backups and exports (Excel, CSV) yourself. The file is only stored where you send it via the share sheet, for example iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email. Backups can be encrypted with a password in the app. The privacy policies of the respective providers apply to these services. We do not receive a copy.

7. No analytics, no advertising, no sharing

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising and no social media integration. We do not share data with third parties because we do not have any. The app is not financial advice; it only calculates with what you enter.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You delete it in the app under More › "Delete all data" or by removing the app from your device. The anonymous purchase identifier at RevenueCat is deleted according to the retention periods applicable there.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not store any personal data about you, these rights are in practice exercised on your device: access and portability via the export and backup functions, deletion as described in section 8. If you have questions, write to haushaltsbuch@bellmann.solutions.

10. This website

The support pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time and requested page in server logs. We use no cookies, no analytics tools and no embedded third-party content on this website. Details in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes

If the app's features change, for example through optional iCloud sync, we will update this policy and point it out in the app.
